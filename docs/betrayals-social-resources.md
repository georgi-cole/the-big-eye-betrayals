# Betrayals Social resource map

Energy measures how much you can do today. Influence pays for persuasion and
political commitments. Info pays for acting on suspicions. All three belong to
the current season. Eyeros are a separate profile currency used by the Store.
[Betrayals monetization](betrayals-monetization.md)

The Energy cap limits new gains. A loaded balance already above the cap is
preserved until spent. Influence and Info carry forward without a cap. Rewarded
ads use the native ad bridge, which is unavailable on GitHub Pages. Skipping or
dismissing an ad before earning its reward leaves the season balance unchanged.

The Round Table ballot, the finale decision, Night role decisions, challenges
and moving the season forward are available with empty Social balances. An
ordinary Round Table contribution also has no wallet cost. Group Chat gives a
way to earn Influence while building rapport with several guests in one move.
It and the other general actions are usable once per game day while available.

## A first-day vote plan without ads or podium rewards

Four check-ins and one Hear a suspicion give 13 Influence and 100 Info. Use the
check-ins to build trust with the contact you intend to approach. Spending this
much Energy before Immunity earns Second Wind even when you finish outside
the podium. If your contact agrees and your target is eligible, you can make
the plan before the Round Table.

| Reality preset | Starting Energy | Social Energy spent before Immunity | Energy after Second Wind | Vote plan cost                  | Balance after plan             |
| -------------- | --------------: | ----------------------------------: | -----------------------: | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Off            |               5 |                                   5 |                        3 | 2 Energy, 12 Influence, 50 Info | 1 Energy, 1 Influence, 50 Info |
| On             |               7 |                                   6 |                        4 | 3 Energy, 12 Influence, 50 Info | 1 Energy, 1 Influence, 50 Info |

The vote plan requires the Reality Mode action unlock or VIP. The Reality
preset controls the Energy allowance and the costs above. Every player can
check in, hear suspicions, take part in challenges and cast a ballot.
